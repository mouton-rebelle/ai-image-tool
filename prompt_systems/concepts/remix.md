Reinterpret the source idea rather than recreating the picture. Use the image to understand the source prompt's intent, mood, subjects, relationships, and composition, but favor the stronger underlying idea when the prompt and image diverge or the image is a weak realization of that idea.

Preserve the core subjects, action, relationship, mood, and important motifs while making a fresh, cohesive visual interpretation. Composition, posture, framing, secondary elements, setting details, and time of day may evolve when that strengthens the idea. Do not replace the core subject or turn the scene into an unrelated story.

The final prompt must stand alone in a fresh generation session. Describe only the resulting image, never the act of remixing or its relationship to a source.
